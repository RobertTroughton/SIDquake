Open-source licenses

SIDquake is built on the work of the C64 community. This page lists the third-party software it includes and the licenses that apply.

SIDquake's in-browser audio engine incorporates libsidplayfp and reSID / reSIDfp, which are licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 2 (or later). Those components are compiled into the WebAssembly binaries served to your browser, so the engine and the C++ sources linked into it are also GPL v2-or-later. The complete corresponding source — including the exact build scripts — is published in the project repository.

SIDquake itself

All SIDquake code — the web app, the WebAssembly analysis modules and the C64 player/visualizer routines — is written by Robert Troughton (Raistlin of Genesis Project), with the exception of the Scrap Columns visualizer, contributed by Scrap. The SIDquake logo was designed by Philip Timmermann (Pepto). Logos and bitmaps in the built-in galleries are the work of their respective C64 artists, each credited by name in the gallery.
